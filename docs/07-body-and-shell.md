# 7 · Body & shell

| Method | Cost | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|------|
| 3D printing (PLA/PETG) | Low (with your own printer) | Precise, repeatable, shareable files | Needs a printer |
| Print service | Medium | No printer needed | Wait time, shipping |
| Cardboard / foam board | Almost free | Fastest prototyping | Fragile |
| Wood / plywood | Low | Warm look, sturdy | Needs tools, less detail |
| Off-the-shelf kits | Medium–high | Designed to fit | Less unique |

## Design tips

- Design around the **display first** — it sets the size of the head.
- Keep **access to the USB port**, and a way to open the shell without breaking it (screws or magnets).
- Add **ventilation** near the board and voltage regulators.
- Use **heat-set inserts** for screws in printed parts.
- Leave ~0.2–0.3 mm clearance for parts that slide together.

## Free tools

- **Design:** FreeCAD, Onshape (free plan), Blender (organic shapes), Tinkercad (beginners).
- **Slicing:** PrusaSlicer, Cura, OrcaSlicer.
